AMENITIES

What We Offer

In Cedar Bluff, AL, Little River Landing is a lakefront marina resort on Weiss Lake, with waterside rooms, wood-fired dining at Woods & Water, and a working marina for boat rentals and fuel-up, right from the dock.

  1. Lodging

    29 lakeside rooms. Port, Starboard, Topside. The water right outside.

  2. Woods & Water Restaurant

    Wood-fired Southern cooking, oysters, and a deck built for sunset.

  3. Water Amenities

    One of two fuel docks on the lake. Pontoons, jet skis, kayaks, eFoils.

  4. Ship’s Store

    Bait, tackle, parts, snacks. Everything the day needs.
